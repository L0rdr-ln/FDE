# Event sounds

Played by `fde::sounds` when `[sounds] enabled = true`. Put your own `startup`, `logout`,
`notification` or `volume` file (`.oga`, `.ogg`, `.opus` or `.wav`) in `~/.local/share/fde/sounds/`
to replace or add one.

| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `notification.ogg` | [Simple Happy Beep.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Simple_Happy_Beep.ogg) (Wikimedia Commons; from freesound.org/people/waveplaySFX/sounds/518147) | waveplaySFX | CC0 1.0 (public domain) |
| `volume.ogg` | the same file, cut to its first quarter second with a fade out | waveplaySFX | CC0 1.0 (public domain) |

`startup` and `logout` are not shipped: no sound with a free licence that fits was found yet.
